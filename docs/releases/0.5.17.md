Canopy 0.5.17 fixes blank terminals on your phone and helps find why the window sometimes freezes and reloads.

### Phone access
- Agent terminals with long histories no longer open blank on your phone. Canopy now sends the latest part of the history in pieces small enough to arrive.
- Turning Remote access off, or changing the PIN, now disconnects phones that are already connected.
- Big pastes from your phone arrive whole instead of dropping the connection.
- A result too large to send to the phone shows a message instead of nothing.
- The QR code and link use this Mac's Tailscale address over http, so phone browsers no longer try https and fail.
- Terminals on your phone keep the prompt in view and switch between Fit width and Readable text, with a cue when more of the line is off to the side. Your Mac's terminal size never changes.
- The Canopy link reconnects after a dropped connection instead of hanging, and a PIN change takes effect on it straight away.

### Your own machines
- Run Canopy on any Linux machine with `npx canopy-host up`, then paste the link it prints into **Add a workspace › Your own machine**. No root or Docker needed; it connects over Tailscale or an SSH tunnel.
- **Add a workspace** has two tabs, Canopy cloud and Your own machine, and shows the hourly price of the size you pick.
- T3 Code machines can no longer be paired or used, and machines paired before are no longer listed.

### Tailscale and team
- Opening another Mac's busy terminal over Tailscale no longer reconnects over and over.
- Sharing a file over 1.5 MB live, or sending more than 2 MB to your team, now says it's too big instead of disconnecting the other person.

### Fixes
- When the window freezes and Canopy reloads it, the log now records what the window was busy with (for example script, layout or memory cleanup). It records only short codes, never content.
- Files outside the project now open from everywhere you can click them (task history, search, research, notes, links from notifications and tabs restored after a restart), not only from terminal links, when Settings → Privacy allows it. Folders that hold credentials stay off limits.
- The "Agent integration required" banner no longer flashes up and clears by itself while your Mac is busy. It shows only if checking keeps failing for about a minute.
- Finished requests are matched more reliably when Canopy checks whether a reply completed them.
