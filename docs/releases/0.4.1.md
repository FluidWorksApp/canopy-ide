Canopy 0.4.1 brings team collaboration and managed remote workspaces into the IDE, alongside the new Build and Engineer workflows.

### IDE workflows
Move between Build and Engineer, manage projects and running agents, and keep your development tools together in one workspace.

### Teams and workspace sharing
Organize people into organizations and teams, invite collaborators, and share workspaces with teams, individuals or everyone in an organization. Choose access roles, project scope and shared resources. Team chat lives inside the IDE.

### Managed remote workspaces
Create and manage cloud workspaces with prebuilt Docker runtimes, persistent files and account settings, and controls to start, stop, hibernate and delete compute. Remote agent, terminal and browser tools work alongside saved sessions and personal or shared accounts.

### Account and workspace experience
Manage your account, balance and workspace access from the IDE and website. Cached loading, clearer permission controls and background progress make workspace management easier to follow.

### Release reliability
Improved Windows installer packaging, stable workspace selection and remote browser streaming. Release publication now waits for every platform and verifies the complete signed updater manifest.
