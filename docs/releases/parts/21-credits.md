### Credit reminders and paused workspaces
Canopy now tells you before your workspace balance runs out. You get an email and a notification in Canopy when your balance drops below $5, when it reaches $0, and a final notice at −$5. Settings → Account shows a banner with how much running time is left, and your spend per workspace over the last 30 days.

If your balance reaches −$10, your running workspaces are stopped and saved, and they can't be started again until credit is added. Your files, snapshots, settings and account are kept, and nothing is deleted. Canopy shows the exact amount needed to start again.

Adding funds isn't available during the preview. Choose **Request more credit** in Settings → Account, or on the message shown when a workspace can't start, and the Canopy team will follow up by email. If you use someone else's shared workspace and their balance runs out, Canopy tells you to ask them to add credit.
