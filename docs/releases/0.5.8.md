Canopy 0.5.8 makes the workspace Remote Desktop more useful, shows every preview embedded, and changes how workspace credit works.

### Remote Desktop
- **Sound:** turn on Sound to hear what is playing in the workspace.
- **Copy and paste:** use the Copy and Paste buttons, or Cmd+C and Cmd+V on a Mac. On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl+Shift+C and Ctrl+V. Emoji and non-Latin text are supported.
- **Minimize:** minimizing keeps the desktop connected, so it is still there when you come back.

### Previews
- Every preview is embedded in Canopy, including apps running in a workspace. Workspace apps reach the preview through a port tunnel.

### Subscriptions
- Canopy can move a session to another of your subscription logins without losing the tab.

### Workspace credit
- New accounts no longer get free credit.
- You need at least $5 of credit to create or start a workspace.
- You get an email when credit falls to 30%, 15% and 5%.
- **A workspace is deleted when credit reaches $0.**

### Update your workspaces
- Existing workspaces need a host update to work with this version. Canopy will prompt you.
