Canopy 0.5.7 says less. Explanations, intro paragraphs and spelled-out numbers are gone from across the app. Details are a hover or a click away.

### A quieter status bar
- CPU and memory are a small live graph. It turns amber or red when resources run low; hover for the numbers, click for the breakdown.
- The brain is a coloured dot. Hover for its name and state.
- Token counts are in the cost chip's tooltip.

### Team memory tidies itself
- Once more than 10 proposals are waiting, repeats are combined and one-task notes are set aside, in the background. There is no button. Nothing is approved for you.
- Each card shows only who proposed it and when.

### Outcomes moved into Usage
- The Outcomes panel left the sidebar. Its numbers are one line in **Usage & cost** in the status bar.

### Shorter everywhere
- Settings descriptions are a few words each, and the notes under them are gone.
- Side panels lost their intro paragraphs, and empty states are a few words.
- Dialogs, the update notice and workspace startup say what happens in one line.
