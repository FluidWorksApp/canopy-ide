Canopy 0.5.4 lets teammates watch terminals in your own projects, simplifies sharing, keeps the companion on your active account, and keeps the Builder ⇄ Engineer switch in one place.

### Share terminals from your own projects
Teammates can now watch a terminal in one of your own projects, not just in projects shared through the workspace. They see it if they have access to that project. **Pair** starts a fresh shell everyone can type in. It's available only for projects shared through the workspace, so a shell others can type in reaches that one project and nothing else.

This needs your cloud workspace to run the 0.5.4 workspace software.

### A simpler Share sheet
Terminals are listed by the names you see on their tabs, such as "claude · SuprFlow · main", instead of their command line or a terminal number. Canopy works out the project from the terminal, so there's no project to pick. A terminal that can't be shared is shown greyed out with the reason. Choose **Share — they can watch** or, where it works, **Pair in a fresh shell**. Shared terminals appear for your team under **Team → Live now**.

### The companion uses your active account
The companion now signs in to its agent CLI with the account selected in **Settings → Accounts**, the same one your sessions and Canopy's other smart features use. Before, it used the CLI's default sign-in, so it could keep failing with "OAuth session expired" while your sessions worked. If its sign-in has expired, the companion offers **Sign in to <CLI>**. On your own computer this opens the sign-in in a terminal. In a cloud workspace it shows the command to run on your computer.

### The mode switch stays put
The **Builder ⇄ Engineer** switch now sits on the right of the top bar in both modes, instead of jumping to the left in Builder mode.

### Clearer workspace updates
When a cloud workspace is too old to update in place, the update notice shows the whole message and no longer offers **Try again**, which couldn't succeed. Other update errors are shown in full.

### Links that are waiting on the server
Until readable links are available on Canopy's servers, the Links section in your account says so quietly instead of showing an error.
