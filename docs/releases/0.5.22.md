Canopy 0.5.22

### New
- Move one agent tab to another profile yourself: right-click the tab and choose **Move to profile**. It is the same move Canopy makes when an account reaches its usage limit. The agent restarts on the chosen account in the same tab and carries on with its conversation, and the other tabs and the app-wide profile stay where they are. Profiles still at their limit are marked, and a profile that isn't signed in to that agent is refused before anything stops.

### Fixes
- Sites you log into in the preview stay logged in after Canopy restarts. Before, a login kept in the page's local storage (most web apps do this) lasted only until you quit. Resetting the preview clears the saved login too. Sites that keep their login in a cookie still won't stay logged in in the preview.
- Opening a pull request into a branch called `production` is no longer stopped as if it were a deploy to production.
