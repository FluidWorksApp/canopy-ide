Canopy 0.4.8 runs the companion and its Slack replies properly from a cloud workspace, lets your agents talk to the agents in a cloud workspace, makes the workspace browser preview much smoother, and keeps a busy workspace from being stopped by hang recovery.

### Companion in cloud workspaces
While you are connected to a cloud workspace, the companion now runs on your computer, as it does locally, instead of failing with "The Linux workspace does not support companion_spawn yet". Its Canopy tools still act on the workspace's projects. Slack messages to the companion are picked up and answered from your desktop again.

### Slack replies from Codex
A companion running on Codex no longer answers Slack with "this session isn't running inside a Canopy terminal". Canopy now passes Codex the connection its Canopy tools need; existing setups pick this up the next time Canopy starts.

### Agents across your cloud workspace
Agents on your desktop can now list the agents running in a cloud workspace, with their names, tasks and status and whether the workspace is online, and message them. Replies come back to the terminal that sent the message, and a message that fails is reported there instead of being dropped. Workspace agents can reply, but cannot yet start a conversation with your desktop agents.

### Smoother workspace browser preview
The workspace browser preview scrolls at about three times the frame rate it did on a typical connection, and each frame is about a third of the size. Fixed a case where the preview could stay blank after connecting.

### Workspace reliability
A workspace that stops responding is now restarted in place, keeping its container and files, and only after 5 minutes without a response. Before, a busy workspace could be stopped after 30 seconds and stay stopped until you resumed it. A workspace you stopped on purpose is never restarted. Teammates' access to a shared workspace no longer lapses after the workspace service restarts.

The agent list, preview, hang recovery and teammate access changes need your workspace's latest host software, installed separately.
