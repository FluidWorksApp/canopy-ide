Canopy 0.4.2 makes sharing a workspace as simple as sharing a document, keeps remote typing responsive, and fixes the workspace start failures from 0.4.1.

### Share a workspace like a document
Share with a person, a team or everyone in your organization, choose Can view or Can edit, and switch Projects, Agent sessions and Accounts on or off. Saving is the share: no setup steps, no copies of your projects, and members reconnect on their own after the workspace restarts. Your home folder and personal accounts are never visible to members.

### Remote workspaces
Workspaces start reliably again: the host service now owns its runtime folder, and every runtime release is booted and checked before it is published. Typing in a remote terminal goes over the live connection instead of one request per key, and Remote access never drops what you type while it reconnects. New workspaces get a 100 GB disk.

### Team chat
Messages appear the moment you send them, with their delivery status. Unread counts show on the Teams icon, each channel and person, and on chat tabs, with a desktop notification when Canopy is in the background.

### Polish
The Workspaces panel no longer jumps on every background refresh.
