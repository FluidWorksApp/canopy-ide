Canopy 0.5.14 adds a local fast model that keeps your request list up to date.

### Local fast model
- Canopy downloads a small model (Laya, 450 MB) after install and runs it on your computer. It is off-switchable in Settings → Brain & memory: off means nothing is downloaded and any copy is deleted.
- It sorts each message and reply quickly; your brain confirms before anything changes.
- The brain dot leaves the status bar when it is off.

### Changes
- The Changes panel no longer has the "Ask an agent" bar.
