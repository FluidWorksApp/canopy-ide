Canopy 0.4.4 gives managed workspaces room to work: choose your storage, grow it later, and let builds and caches use the machine's own disk. Agents can now hand jobs to other workspaces, your other machine and teammates, and restored terminals keep working with the mouse.

### Workspace storage
Choose how much storage a new workspace gets, from 50 GB to 1 TB in 50 GB steps. The first 50 GB are included; more is charged by the hour for as long as the workspace exists. You can grow storage later: the workspace restarts and your files move to the larger disk. Storage can grow but not shrink, and Canopy asks you to confirm that before it changes anything.

### Scratch space
Each workspace now has a private `/scratch` on the machine's own disk. Builds, temporary files and package caches (npm, pip, uv and per-terminal Cargo targets) go there by default, so they no longer fill the storage that holds your projects. Scratch is cleared when the machine is replaced; your files are not.

### Bigger workspaces
A new Max size offers 16 CPUs and 64 GB of memory. Busy workspaces can run far more processes and threads, so browsers, dev servers and agents running together no longer fail with "spawn EAGAIN".

### Agents
Agents can send a job to an agent in another workspace, on your other machine, or on a teammate's, and see each step reported back. Agents opened from the launcher or restored from a session start in their automatic permission mode, the same as task launches, so Codex no longer stops to ask about routine actions.

### Terminals
Mouse wheel and clicks keep working in a terminal after it is restored, both locally and in remote workspaces.

### Workspaces panel
The Workspaces panel is more compact: startup progress fits on one line with its actions beside it, and each person or team you share with fits on a single row. Switching workspaces reuses your saved connection instead of signing in again.
